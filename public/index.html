About

I am a Masters of Architecture candidate at Columbia University's Graduate School of Architecture, Planning and Preservation. My work explores the intersection of structure, materiality, and lived experience — using line, light, and space as primary instruments of design.

Drawing from both computational and hand-drafted traditions, I pursue architecture that is precise yet humane, rigorous yet poetic. My portfolio spans urban housing, cultural institutions, and speculative research into the future of the built environment.
